A paragraph is a fundamental unit of writing that consists of a group of sentences focused on a single theme or idea. It typically includes an introduction (topic sentence), a body (supporting sentences), and a conclusion (closing sentence), which together create unity and coherence. Paragraphs help organize thoughts and improve clarity in writing, making them essential for effective communication in essays, reports, and other forms of written expression. They can vary in length but should always maintain a clear focus on the main idea.
